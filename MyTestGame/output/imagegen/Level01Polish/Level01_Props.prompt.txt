Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: four separate small environment sprites for a Unity 2D top-down pixel-art abandoned-street zombie survival game.

Primary request: redraw the reference chain-link fence and roadside barricades into clean, readable, production-ready 16-bit / 32-bit pixel-art props. Output a 1024x1024 sprite sheet arranged in EXACTLY four equal 512x512 cells, two columns by two rows. Each cell has exactly one isolated complete prop, centered, with generous empty margin and no text. All cells share the same uniform opaque chroma-key hot magenta background RGB(255,0,255). Magenta is used only for the background and never on the props, allowing actual transparent PNG sprites to be extracted afterward.

Reference image role: ExistingPropReference.png shows the current project's old props. Keep their muted weathered gray metal/concrete materials and rusty safety markings, but replace smeared microtextures, disconnected grass and stones, and bulky fuzzy silhouettes with intentional crisp pixel clusters. Do not reproduce the reference's green-gray presentation background or labels.

Top-left cell: a low modular concrete road barricade oriented horizontally across the screen, with a subtle visible top surface and front face, chipped charcoal-gray concrete, three muted rusty-orange and aged cream diagonal hazard stripes, two short integrated concrete feet. Width approximately three times its height. Compact grounded silhouette. No grass, stones, dirt, loose fragments or ground pixels around it.

Top-right cell: a damaged wooden/metal road barricade, horizontal, two worn rusty-orange and aged cream warning boards, two short upright metal legs, one cracked board but all essential parts physically connected. Similar scale and perspective to the first prop. No loose separate fragments, rocks or plant growth.

Bottom-left cell: one reusable east-west chain-link fence module. Two slender upright weathered posts at the left and right edges, a modest top rail, a clear sparse wire diamond mesh between them, with the magenta background visible THROUGH ALL mesh openings. Width about twice its height. Open transparent holes are important. Fence fills about 70% of the cell width, with clean rectangular alignment so adjacent modules can share edge posts. No thick wall, no ground strip, no cast shadow.

Bottom-right cell: a reusable north-south fence module for a top-down map, oriented vertically along the screen. The upright fence is seen almost edge-on from the gameplay camera, producing a slim vertical strip with three modest visible post caps connected by restrained rails/wire detail. Its screen-space height is about three times its width. Upright post highlights remain consistently lit; this must not look like the front-facing fence simply rotated sideways. Keep it physically connected and sparse. No wall, no soil, no ground footprint or shadow.

Style and camera: clean sharp authored pixel art, coherent small pixel clusters, restrained 28-40 color palette for the subjects, no smooth painting or photorealistic noise, no blur, no antialiasing on silhouettes. Orthographic top-down RPG camera with a small readable front face, world axes aligned horizontally/vertically with the screen. Not an isometric diamond view. Same diffuse soft light from above-left, matching neglected muted gray buildings and olive ground. Props are realistically low relative to an adult survivor, not gigantic street furniture.

Constraints: four independent objects, one per cell, consistent scale, no cropping, all touching structural parts connected. Solid uniform magenta background outside silhouettes AND inside wire gaps. No baked ground or contact/cast shadows. No trees, bushes, buildings, vehicles, people, zombies, UI, letters, numbers, captions, logos or watermark.
